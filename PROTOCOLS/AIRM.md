# AIRM — AI Resilience Mesh Protocol (draft v0.1)

## Purpose

Federated exchange of **signed** behavioural observations, minimised threat indicators, test results, attack signatures, and sandboxed research workloads among independent nodes.

## Participants

Individuals, enterprises, hospitals, universities, cloud providers, researchers, and public-interest organisations.

## Trust model

Multiple intelligence providers, competing policy authorities, and independent implementations—reducing capture of the defensive mesh itself.

## Grid workloads (Phase 8)

Signed, sandboxed, resource-limited, reproducible research tasks—never arbitrary privileged code on contributor nodes.

## Next steps

Node authentication, gossip or hub federation options, and privacy-preserving indicator formats.
