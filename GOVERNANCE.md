# AI-CERTAIN Governance (v0.1)

## Intent

AI-CERTAIN is an **open protocol and reference implementation ecosystem**, not a central global operator.

## Founding charter

AI-CERTAIN is an open, non-exclusive effort to develop interoperable mechanisms that constrain, observe, attribute and revoke AI-enabled capabilities. It will not:

- establish a political authority;
- determine ideological truth; or
- provide a single global shutdown mechanism.

Technical controls shall be evidence-based, auditable, contestable and capable of independent implementation.

## Recommended mechanisms

- Open specification with transparent change control
- Multiple independent implementations
- Reproducible builds and signed releases
- Public security review and vulnerability disclosure
- Conflict-of-interest declarations
- Independent technical stewardship
- Explicit limits on unilateral emergency authority

## Capture resistance

No participant should use the protocol to impose political censorship through technical reputation scores. Threat signals describe **observable technical behaviour and evidence**, not ideological conformity.

## RiskLine relationship

RiskLine should **not** own AI-CERTAIN. Its natural role is **continuous assurance and evidence** (control mapping, runtime verification, regulatory overlays) on top of vendor-neutral infrastructure.

## Standards engagement

Composable with NIST agent identity work, OWASP agentic security, C2PA provenance, and eventual IETF protocol work—without claiming endorsement from those bodies.
